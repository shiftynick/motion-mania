# Motion Mania launch film

Audience: builders who want an agent to make motion videos from a product repo.
Message: turn a brief and editable code into a video, then inspect and improve it.
Deliverables: 15 seconds, 30 fps, vertical and landscape MP4, poster and review bundle.

Visual direction: oversized typography, warm paper, charcoal and signal orange.
Motion: purposeful scale changes, moving editorial layouts, staggered typography.
Audio: original synthesized 120 BPM score; scene changes land on its beat grid.
