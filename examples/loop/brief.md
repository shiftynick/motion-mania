# The loop — Motion Mania, start to finish

A film about Motion Mania itself, made to exercise every part of the toolkit at once: storyboard intent and intended holds, narration with word-highlighted captions, an original score with cut accents, picture and audio checks, two formats, and rounds of review by a fresh critic agent.

Audience: developers who already ship with coding agents and have seen generated video that looks fine in one frame and falls apart in motion. Benefit to show: the agent does not just make a film; it plans it, inspects what actually rendered, and has a second agent check the work.

Deliverables: 36 seconds, 30 fps, 1920×1080 and 1080×1920 MP4s with narration and burned-in captions.

Organizing idea: one orange frame carries through the whole loop. It starts as a highlight around a line of the brief, becomes the selected cell of the storyboard, the live preview beside the code, the selected contact-sheet frame, the marker on each critic finding, and finally splits into the two delivered formats.

Visual direction: warm paper (#f3f0e7), ink (#191b19), Motion Mania orange (#ff5a2b) for the frame and cuts only. Evidence panels use the dark style of the tool's real waveform and activity charts. The critic is blue; verified fixes are green. Inter for type and interface, JetBrains Mono for code and commands. One headline position per format, held for every shot.

Motion: the frame travels with a fast exit and a slow landing; destinations are placed before it arrives. Every action produces a visible result: a command yields its artifact, a code line yields its motion, a critic check yields a stamp.

Narration: a patient engineer explaining a tool to a colleague. Full sentences, no slogans or statistics, no claims about outcomes. The last four seconds are silent so the wordmark can land.
Captions: word-highlighted, from a transcript of the final take, corrected by hand.
Audio: original 120 BPM score with cuts on the beat; sparse to open, fuller under the evidence, lighter under the critic, a resolving chord on the wordmark. One soft whoosh per real transition, small clean ticks only on visible actions. Target -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.

The interface, file contents, findings, and numbers on screen are a designed illustration of the workflow, not a recording of a real session.
