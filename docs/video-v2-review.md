# Motion Mania v2 production review

Source: `examples/launch-v2`. First version preserved in `examples/launch`.

The 15-second revision replaces separate explanatory cards with one persistent graphic surface: slash → code panel → motion study → review frame → export. It uses larger headlines, deliberate holds, an orange closing frame, distinct portrait composition, and an original stereo score. Both outputs are Full HD at 30 fps.

## Inspection and changes

- Inspected landscape and portrait contact sheets, a portrait phone preview, and the landscape format shot at full resolution.
- Inspected adjacent-frame strips around the code-panel arrival (2.5s), review fan (6.5s), and portrait closing wipe (12s). These show sampled transitions, not a full playback review.
- Fixed future cards appearing early because GSAP `fromTo` immediately applied opacity 1. Future elements now start at opacity 0.
- Removed the small art caption during the aspect conversion so it cannot collide with the format label. Counter-scaled the monogram, disc, and label while the surrounding surface changes shape.
- Corrected the illustrated tall surface to 9:16. The landscape and portrait output files use their actual configured dimensions.
- Hid the prior composition after the orange wipe covers it. Intentional graphic cropping is annotated on the orbit shapes; text collision checks remain active.

Final evidence: `examples/launch-v2/reviews/landscape-eUItnc` and `vertical-QXxjYH`. Stable contact sheets and posters are copied into `examples/launch-v2/out`. These generated artifacts are locally available and ignored by Git.

## Technical evidence

Both MP4 exports were probed: 15 seconds, 30 fps, required audio present, 1920×1080 landscape and 1080×1920 portrait. Frames decoded from each final MP4 were visually inspected. Both formats passed HyperFrames runtime/layout/contrast checks with no errors. Seven static advice warnings remain for the dense track and suggested sub-compositions. Each format's three forward/reverse seek comparisons matched. Reports: `verify-landscape-GAN5gv` and `verify-vertical-uP2exw` under the reviews directory. This is sampled, single-machine evidence.

The score is generated by `examples/launch-v2/scripts/score.mjs` using seeded synthesis, then normalized with FFmpeg. Normalization analysis reports -16.2 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP. Audio listening and full video playback were not performed; presence and measurements do not establish artistic sound quality. Source audio provenance is in `assets/manifest.json`.

The code is an explanatory illustration, not a captured product interface. Secondary code and labels are small in contact sheets; the headlines communicate the main narrative. A real product film should substitute observable product behavior or authorized footage where appropriate.

## Improvements carried into the toolkit

`motion-mania plan` validates storyboard coverage and returns questions about missing shot intent. Storyboards can record viewer goals, focal points, transitions, sound, and explicit review times. `review` captures those moments and automatic cut strips, then creates a critique organized by shot. The skill now includes practical creative-direction guidance and this worked example. No numerical creative score or automated approval is claimed.
